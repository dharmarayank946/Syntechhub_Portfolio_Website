MY STORY

From Curiosity to Creating for the Web

When I started college, I did not know much about technology or development. Through my college projects and hackathons, I gradually started exploring different areas and began with frontend development. I enjoyed creating websites, and this made me more interested in Web Development.

An opportunity at Mastertej became an important step in my journey. During my 45-day internship, I gained practical exposure to website development, UI design, project management, research, documentation and AI-assisted workflows. This experience helped me understand that web development is more than just frontend development and encouraged me to explore Full Stack Development.

I continue to learn different areas, including Python, but Web Development remains my main focus. At the same time, my creative side led me toward UI/UX. Now, in my 5th semester, I have started learning Figma and UI/UX concepts so that I can combine creativity with technology.
